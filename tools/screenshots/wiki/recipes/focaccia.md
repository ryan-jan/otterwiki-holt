# Focaccia

A slow, overnight focaccia with a crisp base and an open crumb.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Strong white flour | 500 g |
| Water | 400 g |
| Salt | 10 g |
| Instant yeast | 4 g |
| Olive oil | Plenty |

## Method

1. Mix everything except the oil until no dry flour is left.
2. Fold the dough four times, 30 minutes apart.
3. Leave it in the fridge overnight.
4. Tip it into an oiled tin, dimple it and bake at 230°C for 25 minutes.

> [!NOTE]
> Rosemary and flaky salt on top are not optional.
